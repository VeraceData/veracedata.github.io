The process

Four steps. Every engagement.

You know exactly what to expect before you start.

1

We have an initial conversation

You tell us about your study. Which diagnoses you are working with, how many sites are involved, and what your concern is. No commitment required. We walk you through what we need and what the report will contain.

What you need for this call: A general sense of your study design and which diagnoses are involved. Nothing technical. No data.
2

You give us access to your study data

Your team already has data access agreements in place with your participating sites. We work with your data team to access the OMOP data for each site in your study. No patient-level data is required.

Note: We do not contact your hospital sites directly or require anything from them. All data coordination goes through your team.
3

We score the divergence across all concepts

We compare the summary profiles from each site for every concept in your study. Every concept receives a divergence score. The analysis is systematic and repeatable. The same inputs always produce the same result.

No black box: The report includes a full written breakdown behind every score, not just the final number. You see exactly what is driving the divergence for each concept.
4

You receive a plain-language divergence report

For every concept in your study you receive a complete report. Not just a number. A written explanation of what is different, why it matters for your specific study, and exactly what to do about it. Every decision is recorded in a tamper-evident audit log that travels with the report.

Turnaround: Typically one week from first call to final report. For tighter timelines, ask us on the call.

What you find out

Three possible outcomes for each concept

Every concept in your study is classified based on how consistently the sites apply it in practice.

Divergent

The two sites define this concept differently and we know exactly how and why. The study query is blocked until a qualified reviewer on your team documents the resolution.

Coherent

Both sites define this concept consistently. Safe for pooled analysis without adjustment. The query proceeds immediately.

Needs review

Divergence detected but further expert review is recommended before pooling. Your designated reviewer receives the full evidence package.

Before you call

Questions we hear every time

Answers to what every research team asks before booking a first call.

Do the hospitals need to hire anyone or change anything?
No. We work with the data your team already has access to through your existing site agreements. No new staff, no new infrastructure, and no new permissions required at the hospital level. The review and resolution happen at your organization.
Who decides which hospital is right when they differ?
The system does not decide who is right. Both hospitals may be coding correctly for their own clinical workflows. What the report tells you is that they are different and exactly how, so a qualified person on your team can make that call deliberately, on the record, before the study runs.
We already do data quality checks. How is this different?
Data quality checks catch missing values, out-of-range numbers, and formatting errors. In our heart failure example both hospitals passed every data quality check. This measures something different: whether two institutions are applying the same concept the same way in clinical practice. No existing data quality tool answers that question.
We use OMOP. Does standardization not solve this?
OMOP standardizes the vocabulary, not how institutions apply it in practice. Both hospitals in our example used the correct OMOP concept code. The divergence was in the clinical workflow behind the code.
What if we have more than two sites?
The system handles multiple sites. Each site contributes a summary profile and the analysis scores divergence across all site combinations. The report covers every concept across every site pairing in your study.
