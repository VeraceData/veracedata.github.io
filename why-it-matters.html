Why it matters

The difference between a study that holds up and one that does not

Undetected concept gaps are one of the most common hidden causes of multi-site study failure. Most teams never catch them because no existing tool looks for them.

The stakes

What happens when no one checks

Two hospitals. Same concept code. Completely different patients. You pool the data. Your results are wrong. The only sign is that the numbers look slightly off.

Studies redesigned midway

A study redesigned midway costs significantly more than one designed correctly from the start, in time, budget, and delayed submissions.

Results that cannot be defended

If a regulatory reviewer asks whether concept gaps were assessed before the study ran, without this documentation the answer is no.

Sites blamed for their coding

When results are questioned, hospital data gets scrutinized. Without documentation there is no way to show the difference was known and accounted for before the study ran.

Invisible to existing tools

Data quality dashboards catch formatting errors and missing values. They do not measure whether two sites are applying the same concept the same way in practice.

Patient privacy

No patient data leaves any site. Ever.

The analysis is designed so that no patient information can be transmitted. Privacy protection is built into how the system works, not added on top of it.

No patient identifiers

Person-level information is never part of the analysis at any stage.

No raw source codes

The analysis works from summary profiles, not underlying records or diagnosis codes.
